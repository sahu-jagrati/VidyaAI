To solve mirror and water image questions easily we
can use the following trick-
Trick:The part of the figure which is nearer to mirror
will appear first and the part which is far from the
mirror will appear later in the image.
Now eliminate those options which do not follow this
trick. For e.g.
1.

was farthest from the mirror, it appeared last.

Vertical Symmetry:
If after cutting a picture vertically from the middle
we get same left and right parts of the picture, it is
called Vertical Symmetry.

PAPER FOLDING AND CUTTING
In the questions related to this topic a paper is shown
by dotted line which tells us how the paper is folded or
cut. Then we have to answer that after folding or unfold-
ing what figure is formed.
While solving these questions we use the same tricks
given in mirror and water image chapter. We unfold the given
paper one by one and form the images of them.
If we unfold the paper vertically, then we get mirror
image. If we unfold the paper horizontally, then we get
water image. Also if we unfold the paper diagonally, we
get diagonal image.
Let’s understand it with the help of following ex-
amples:
